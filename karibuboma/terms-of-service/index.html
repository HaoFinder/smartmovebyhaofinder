6. Verification System

KaribuBoma uses a 3-Tier Verification System to build trust and ensure safety:

  • Tier 1 (Basic): Phone and SMS verification. Enables browsing hosts and public events.
  • Tier 2 (Verified Boma): M-Pesa name/ID match and National ID upload. Enables unlimited messaging and verified badge.
  • Tier 3 (SmartMove Trusted): Physical address verification by SmartMove agents. Enables featured host badge and priority matching.

Verification is required to message Hosts, book stays, and access certain Platform features. SmartMove.Co.Ke reserves the right to require verification at any time.
